## Generated, not hand-edited

Prose is authored in `../scripts/make_kais_main.py`. Every table and figure is
lifted **verbatim** from `../paper-kbs/kbs-article.tex` by
`../scripts/extract_floats.py`, so the numbers remain the ones
`check_paper_numbers.py` validates against `artefacts/`. The restructure
therefore cannot introduce a transcription error: it can only move a float or
fail to move it, and `tests/test_kais_split.py` checks that nothing is lost.

Run `make regen` after editing the generator. Do not edit the `.tex` files.

## Against the KAIS constraints

| Constraint | Ported draft | Now |
|---|---|---|
| 15,000 words | ~20,100 | **~5,500** |
| Display items | 21 | **10** (6 tables, 3 figures, 1 algorithm) |
| Abstract 150--250 words | ~280 | **~230** |
| Headings, max 3 levels | complies | complies |
| Numbered `[n]` citations | yes | yes |

The word count is well under the ceiling rather than near it. That is
deliberate: the playbook targeted 14,000, but the argument does not need
14,000 words once the rebuttal voice, the triple-hedged caveats and the
robustness narration are removed. Padding back to the target would restore
exactly the prose that made the draft hard to review. Everything cut is in the
ESM, not deleted.

## What is new relative to the KBS version

- **Fig. 8, sampling error.** Permutation sampling at $M=500$ has a
  95th-percentile error of 2.73% of $v(\mathcal{G})$, against a seed-to-seed
  spread of 2.22% and a materiality threshold of 1.91%. Exactness is
  load-bearing, not decorative. Artefact `artefacts/sampling_error.json`.
- **Title** no longer leads with the coined method name.
- **Abstract** carries no machine-epsilon residual.
- `seq` is described as PPMI--SVD co-occurrence with a symmetric matrix, and
  the text says explicitly that it is not a sequential model in the SASRec
  sense.
